Before this drives decisions. Celina §C.9 reads "differential grades shall not exceed ten percent" — a grade-break limit, matching how the same manual uses "grade differential" in its profile requirements (§4.02: "grade differentials greater than 1.2% require vertical curves"). No maximum residential driveway grade appears anywhere in the 2024 standards. Get the City Engineer to confirm that reading in writing before construction decisions rest on it.
